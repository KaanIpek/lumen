LUMEN — DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

LUMEN, published by RLD Games. Last updated: 17 August 2026
You almost certainly do not have one. LUMEN needs no account to play, and it never asks you to make one. An account exists only if you deliberately signed in to post a score to the shared leaderboard. Everything else — your scores, your shards, every item you unlocked — lives on your own device and works signed out.

Deleting it from inside the app

This is the whole process, and it takes one tap. Nothing is emailed, nobody has to approve it, and it happens immediately:

  1. Open LUMEN
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Under ACCOUNT, tap DELETE ACCOUNT
  4. Confirm

What is deleted

  • Your leaderboard entry — the display name you typed and the score attached to it
  • Your account itself, including the identifier your sign-in provider gave us

Both go immediately and permanently. We keep no copy, no backup row and no archive of them, so there is nothing left to retain and no retention period to state. We cannot restore any of it afterwards — not on request, because it is genuinely gone.

What is kept

Nothing on our side. On your device the game keeps playing exactly as it did: best score, shards, unlocked skins, trails, maps, missions, settings. That data never left your device in the first place, which is why deleting the account does not touch it. To erase that too, use Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app.

If you cannot open the app

Email rld.ranger07@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, or tell us the display name shown on the leaderboard, and we will delete the account and its row for you.
