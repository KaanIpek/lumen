# LUMEN — Press Kit / Fact Sheet

## Fact sheet

- **Title:** LUMEN
- **Tagline:** *flip · thread · flow*
- **Genre:** Hypercasual arcade / endless high-score
- **Platforms:** Web (desktop + mobile), installable PWA. Steam and Android/iOS
  builds are wired and buildable from the same source
- **Price:** Free, with cosmetic-only purchases (no ads, no pay-to-win)
- **Input:** One button — tap / click / space · optional gamepad · optional voice
- **Players:** Single-player
- **Engine:** Vanilla JavaScript + Canvas 2D (no dependencies)
- **Audio:** 100% synthesised at runtime (Web Audio API — no sample files)
- **Session length:** 20–90 seconds
- **Languages:** English, Türkçe, Español, 中文

## Elevator pitch (25 words)

You're a spark of light. Tap to flip gravity, thread neon gaps, and chain the
glow into a slow-motion flow state. Three-second learn, endless mastery.

## Short description (store, ~50 words)

LUMEN is a one-thumb neon arcade game. Tap to flip gravity and weave a falling
spark of light through a corridor of glowing gates. Grab motes to build a combo,
then tip into **flow state** — bullet-time, drenched in colour, scored by a
live synthwave soundtrack. Easy to learn. Impossible to put down.

## Long description (store)

A dark corridor. A single spark of light. One rule: **tap to flip gravity.**

LUMEN turns that one input into a game you'll chase for weeks. Thread razor-thin
gaps by timing your flips. Dive for glowing motes to stack a combo multiplier —
and get greedy, because chaining sixteen tips you into **flow state**: the whole
world drops into slow-motion, the palette ignites magenta, and an adaptive
synthwave soundtrack swells to full. That's the moment you'll want to share.

Every tap is answered with particles, light-trails, screen shake, and a synth
note that rises with your streak. The difficulty ramps fair and readable, from a
gentle open to moving gates and hair's-breadth gaps. One hit ends the run — and
sends you straight back for "one more".

No downloads to start, no dependencies, no filler. Just pure, luminous,
one-more-run arcade.

**Features**
- One-thumb gravity-flip gameplay — pure skill
- **Seven game modes**: Classic, Vortex (the world turns as you fly), Mirror
  (everything runs backwards), Sprint, Blackout (light arrives in pulses),
  Precision, and Zen (nothing can kill you). Each keeps its own record
- Flow state: slow-motion bullet-time you *earn*
- Combo multiplier up to ×12 with a greed-driving decay timer
- Four gate archetypes: static, moving, breathing and twin double-gaps
- Six worlds to unlock, 25 orb skins and 13 light trails — four skins can't be
  bought at any price, only earned
- 26 achievements, five skill lines, and items you carry into a run and fire by
  hand — from a button, the number keys, or by saying *"shield"*
- Rotating missions, a local top-10 leaderboard, an optional online board,
  and a paid revive
- Daily Challenge: the same course for every player, every day, with a streak —
  and immune to every upgrade, item and difficulty setting
- Easy / Normal / Hard, so it meets you where you are
- Reactive neon art + fully synthesised adaptive soundtrack
- Procedural difficulty, share-image cards, offline play
- Built for phones: touch, haptics, responsive, installable
- Accessibility: colour-vision presets, high contrast, reduce-flashing,
  `prefers-reduced-motion`, screen-reader score announcements
- Four languages, and privacy-first: opt-in telemetry, no ad SDK

## Design pillars

1. **Instant to understand** — one input, one rule.
2. **Impossible to put down** — one-hit death + always-reachable high score.
3. **Feels incredible** — juice on every action; flow is the signature moment.
